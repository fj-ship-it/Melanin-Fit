Be honest: who eats better? 1 or 2

1 has two 24-oz glasses of sweet tea at dinner. "It's homemade."
2 has ice water with dinner and one glass of sweet tea on Sunday.

A lot of family recipes use 1 cup of sugar per gallon, some use more. That's about 12 g of sugar in every 8 oz, so two 24-oz glasses come to about 75 g (1 cup of sugar is about 200 g, USDA).
The American Heart Association suggests men keep added sugar to about 36 g a day. Homemade is still sugar.
The answer is 2.
