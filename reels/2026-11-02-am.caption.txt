Couch Dad asked: "Why do my knees hurt on the stairs?"

Kingston's 3 answers:
1. Every pound counts. In a study of adults with knee arthritis, each pound lost took about 4 pounds of load off the knee with every step (Messier et al., 2005).
2. Build your thighs. Stronger thigh muscles help support the knee. Start with 10 chair sit-to-stands a day.
3. Keep moving, go easy. Exercise like walking, biking and swimming is a first-line treatment for knee arthritis pain (American College of Rheumatology).

Swelling, locking, or pain that won't go away? Get it checked by your doctor.
