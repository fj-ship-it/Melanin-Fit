5 signs it might not be "just snoring."

Loud snoring. Gasping or choking at night. Morning headaches. Sleepy all day. Blood pressure that won't come down.
These are common signs of sleep apnea, where your breathing stops and starts while you sleep (AASM).
Only a doctor can tell. Ask about a sleep study. Some can be done at home.
