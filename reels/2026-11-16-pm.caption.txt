A banana isn't even top 5 for potassium.

Be honest: who eats better for blood pressure? 1 or 2

Potassium helps your body get rid of sodium and eases tension in your blood vessel walls.
Per serving, white beans, a baked potato, spinach, plain yogurt and sweet potato all beat the banana.
Men need about 3,400 mg a day. Get it from food first.
Kidney issues or on blood pressure meds? Talk to your doctor before loading up on potassium or salt substitutes.
(Sources: Dietary Guidelines for Americans food sources table, NIH ODS, American Heart Association)
