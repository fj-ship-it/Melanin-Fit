4 fat-loss myths a lot of us still believe 👇

1. "Sweating more burns more fat." Sweat is your body cooling off. The scale drop is water, and it comes back when you drink.
2. "Detox teas flush out fat." Many work as laxatives. Your liver and kidneys already handle the detox.
3. "Brown sugar is the healthy one." It's white sugar with a little molasses, with nearly the same calories (USDA).
4. "Crunches burn belly fat." You can't spot-reduce fat. Belly fat comes off as overall body fat comes down.

What works isn't a hack: move every day, eat mostly whole foods, sleep 7+ hours.
