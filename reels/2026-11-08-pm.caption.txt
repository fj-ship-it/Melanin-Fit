Your body has favorite foods. Sunday dinner edition 🍽️

Collard greens · bones: vitamin K, which your body uses for healthy bones (NIH ODS)
Sweet potatoes · eyes: beta-carotene, which your body turns into vitamin A for vision (NIH ODS)
Salmon · heart: omega-3 fats. The AHA recommends fish twice a week
Black-eyed peas · gut: fiber that feeds the good bacteria in your gut
Eggs · muscles: about 6 g of protein each (USDA)
Oranges · skin: vitamin C, which your body needs to make collagen (NIH ODS)
