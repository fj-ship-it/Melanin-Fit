Couch Dad asked: "Is fruit sugar the same as soda sugar?"

1. An apple comes with fiber. A medium apple has about 19 g of sugar and 4 g of fiber (USDA). Fiber slows it down and helps you feel full.
2. Soda is all added sugar. A 12-oz can of regular cola has about 39 g (label) and zero fiber. The American Heart Association says men should stay under 36 g of added sugar a day.
3. Eat your fruit. Don't drink your sugar. The Dietary Guidelines don't count the sugar in whole fruit as added sugar. Soda's counts.
