Be honest: who eats better? 1 or 2 👇

1 — Hoops twice a week. 3 sports drinks a day "for hydration." One more bottle at the court.
2 — Lifts 3 days a week. Water all day. One sports drink after a long, sweaty game.

The catch: a 20-oz sports drink has about 34 g of sugar (check the label). Three is about 100 g. The American Heart Association says men should keep added sugar under 36 g a day. For most workouts, water does the job.
Answer: 2.
