Be honest: who eats better? 1 or 2 👇

1 — Vanilla yogurt and granola every morning, "for my gut." Another vanilla yogurt cup at 3 PM. Water all day.
2 — Plain yogurt and blueberries. A small handful of peanuts. Coffee with one spoon of sugar.

The catch: low-fat vanilla yogurt has about 23 g of sugar per 6 oz. Plain has about 12 g, and that's the natural sugar in milk (USDA). Two vanilla cups is about 47 g before the granola. Go plain and add your own fruit.
Answer: 2.
