Couch Dad asks: "Why do I crash at 3 PM?"

Part of that afternoon dip is your body clock. Lunch can make it worse.
1) Add protein + fiber at lunch (chicken, beans, greens), not just fries and white bread.
2) Drink water through the day. Even mild dehydration can leave you feeling tired.
3) Walk 10 minutes after lunch. Short walks after meals can help blunt blood sugar spikes (Sports Medicine review, 2022).
Bonus: sleep. Adults need 7+ hours a night (CDC). Tired all the time no matter what? Get checked by your doctor.
